About Camstays

Your project deserves a clear plan.

Camstays is a professional construction and renovation project management company supporting residential and commercial clients. We help bring people, priorities and project activities together through practical planning and consistent oversight.

From defining the scope to coordinating trades and monitoring progress, our focus is to make the process more organized, transparent and manageable.

  • Structured planning and scheduling
  • Contractor and stakeholder coordination
  • Progress, cost and issue monitoring
  • Clear communication from start to handover
